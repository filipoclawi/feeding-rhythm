About records & updates

Checked is when the private source was checked for this published snapshot; Observed is the latest recorded data. Publication can take a few minutes. Browser refreshes check the published file every minute, not the private source.

Left and Right tags name the recorded side; each row shows start–end and nursing minutes. An end is calculated only from a recorded start and known duration. — means unknown; + and Partial mark an incomplete total. Order unknown means list order is not a sequence. Uncertain marks a record with timing uncertainty. The last recorded side is not a suggestion for the next feed.

Grouped sessions combine starts within 60 minutes of the first start: this is inferred, not certain, and does not extend through a chain of later starts. Totals sum known nursing minutes, not elapsed wall time. Only the latest three grouped sessions show individual detail; older records are coarse daily aggregates.

Not all feeds may be recorded. These observations are not a feeding schedule or medical advice. Exact recent public records can be copied and retained by visitors.

Totals sum known feeding minutes, including partial sessions; averages use fully timed sessions only. The interval averages consecutive start-to-start gaps, including overnight gaps, assigned to the later start’s day; it is not time spent feeding. Missing values are shown as —, never as zero. Historical individual events are not displayed. Historical durations are rounded to 5 minutes and intervals to half-hours.
